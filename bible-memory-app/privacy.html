Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Bible Memory App ("Company," "We," "Us," or "Our") operates the Bible Memory App mobile application (the "Service").

This Privacy Policy explains how We collect, use, disclose, and safeguard Your information when You use the Service and describes Your privacy rights under applicable laws including:

  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA)
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA)

By using the Service, You agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Data

Users may optionally create an account using an email address and password. This information is stored securely via Firebase Authentication and is used solely to sync your saved memory verses across devices.

User Content

Bible verses, memory notes, and practice progress you save within the app are stored locally on your device and, if you are signed in, synced to your private account in Cloud Firestore. This data is only accessible to you.

Audio Data

When you request audio playback of a verse, the verse text is sent to a Google Cloud Text-to-Speech service to generate an audio file. The resulting audio is cached on Cloudflare R2 and on your device for future playback. No personal information is included in this request.

Usage Data (Automatically Collected)

Certain limited technical information may be collected automatically by the iOS or Android operating system or hosting infrastructure, such as:

  • IP address
  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash diagnostics
  • Basic usage metrics (e.g., app launch time, feature access)

This data is used solely for maintaining app functionality and security. It is not used for advertising, sold, or used for behavioral profiling.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain the Service
  • Sync your memory verses across devices (if signed in)
  • Generate verse audio on demand
  • Improve stability and performance
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Ensure security
We do not use data for advertising, behavioral tracking, selling user data, or profiling users.

3. Third-Party Services

The Service uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore (Google) — for optional account creation and data sync
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech — to generate verse audio on demand
  • Cloudflare R2 — to cache generated verse audio files

Each service is governed by its own privacy policy. We do not use third-party advertising networks or cross-app tracking technologies.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If You are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process data under:

  • Legitimate Interests — maintaining and improving app functionality
  • Contract Performance — providing sync features for signed-in users
  • Legal Obligation — complying with applicable laws

5. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to:

  • Know what Personal Information is collected
  • Request deletion
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Opt out of sale or sharing
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information
  • Non-discrimination for exercising rights

Categories collected in the last 12 months:

  • Identifiers (email address for account holders, IP address, device identifiers)
  • Internet activity information (basic app usage data)
  • User-generated content (saved verses and notes)
We do not sell Personal Information. We do not share Personal Information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. CalOPPA Disclosures

The Service does not respond to Do Not Track (DNT) signals. However, we do not engage in cross-site tracking or behavioral advertising.

7. Data Retention

Account data is retained until you delete your account. Usage data may be retained for up to 24 months for security and diagnostics purposes. Cached audio files are retained indefinitely to reduce repeat processing costs but contain no personal information.

8. Data Security

We use commercially reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children.

10. International Data Transfers

If data is processed outside Your jurisdiction, We implement appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "Last updated" date will reflect the most recent revision.
