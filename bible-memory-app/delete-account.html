Delete Your Account & Data

We respect your right to control your personal data. You can request deletion of your Bible Memory App account and all associated data using either of the methods below.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app (instant)

The fastest way to delete your account is directly inside the app:

  • Open the Bible Memory App
  • Go to Settings
  • Tap Account
  • Tap Delete Account and confirm

This immediately deletes your account and all synced verse data from our servers.

Option 2 — Submit a deletion request by email

If you no longer have access to the app or prefer to submit a request by email, contact us at the address below. Please include the email address associated with your account.

Email us to request account deletion:

travisprefersdosequis@gmail.com

Request Deletion by Email
We will process your deletion request within 30 days. Once deleted, your account, saved verses, and all synced data cannot be recovered.

What gets deleted

  • Your account credentials (email address)
  • All saved memory verses and progress synced to your account
  • Reminder settings and preferences stored in your account

What is not deleted

  • Locally stored data on your device — uninstalling the app removes this
  • Anonymized usage logs retained for security purposes (up to 24 months, per our Privacy Policy)
