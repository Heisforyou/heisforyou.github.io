Frequently Asked Questions

How many games can I hide?

As many as you want. Tap the shield on any game. Every game you do not hide keeps updating live.

How do I see a hidden score again?

Tap the hidden game and confirm that you want to reveal it. The score comes back for that game only.

Why are scores not updating?

Live scores are loaded from the internet. Check that the phone has a connection, then leave the league open for a moment and try again.

A league says it is out of season.

That league has no current slate, so its row stays visible but cannot be opened. It becomes available again when games are scheduled.

Why can't I take a screenshot?

While any game is hidden, screenshots and the app-switcher preview are blocked so a glance at recent apps cannot leak a score. Reveal every hidden game and screenshots work again.

Do I need an account?

No. Hidden games and display settings stay on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes them.
