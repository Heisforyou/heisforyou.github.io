Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Going Dark ("We," "Us," or "Our") operates the Going Dark mobile application (the "Service"), also listed as "Going Dark: Hide One Score."

This Privacy Policy explains how We handle information when You use the Service and describes Your privacy rights under applicable laws including:

  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA)
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA)

By using the Service, You agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Data

We do not require You to create an account.

We do not collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Payment information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Precise or coarse location

If You email Us, We receive the address You write from and whatever You include in the message.

Information stored on Your device

The Service saves a few preferences on Your phone or tablet so they are still there the next time You open the app. That information stays on the device. We do not receive it, and We have no server that stores it.

On the device, the Service may save:

  • Which game You chose to hide, including the league, the two team names, and the time You hid it
  • Display preferences, such as accent colors and the order in which leagues are listed

Hidden games do not include the score. Uninstalling the Service, or clearing its storage in system settings, deletes this information from the device.

Scoreboard requests

To show live scores, the Service requests public scoreboard data from ESPN at site.api.espn.com. Your device sends that request directly. A request can include the league, a date, and, for college sports, a conference or division filter.

ESPN receives the IP address of the network You are using and ordinary connection details such as device and browser information that accompany an HTTPS request. We do not send ESPN Your name, email address, or the fact that You hid a particular game. ESPN's own privacy policy describes what ESPN does with information it receives.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

2. How We Use Information

  • Show live scores for the leagues You open
  • Remember, on Your device, the game You hid and the display preferences You chose
  • Reply if You contact Us by email

We do not use information for advertising, behavioral tracking, selling user data, or profiling.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If You are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland:

  • Scoreboard requests are sent so We can provide the Service You asked for
  • Preferences stored on Your device are processed on that device to provide the Service, and are not transmitted to Us
  • If You email Us, We use that message to respond to You

4. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to:

  • Know what Personal Information is collected
  • Request deletion
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Opt out of sale or sharing
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information
  • Non-discrimination for exercising rights

We do not collect Personal Information through the Service. Preferences and hidden-game choices remain on Your device. We do not sell Personal Information, and We do not share Personal Information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Because scoreboard requests go from Your device to ESPN, ESPN may receive an IP address and related connection data. We do not receive that information back from ESPN.

To exercise a privacy right related to an email You sent Us, contact the address below. We cannot delete on-device app data remotely. You can remove it by clearing the Service's storage or uninstalling it.

5. CalOPPA Disclosures

The Service does not respond to Do Not Track (DNT) signals. We do not track You across other apps or websites.

We do not use third-party advertising networks or cross-app tracking technologies.

6. Data Retention

Information stored by the Service remains on Your device until You change it, clear the Service's storage, or uninstall the Service.

If You email Us, We keep the message long enough to respond and to keep a record of the request, then delete it when We no longer need it.

7. Data Security

On-device information is protected by Your device's operating system. Scoreboard requests use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children. If You believe a child has emailed Us, contact Us and We will delete that message.

9. International Data Transfers

Scoreboard requests are sent to ESPN, and email You send Us may be handled by email providers, in the United States or another country. Those providers' safeguards are described in their own privacy policies.

10. App Stores

If You install the Service from Google Play or the Apple App Store, Google or Apple processes Your store account under that company's privacy policy. That processing is separate from the Service.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date will show the latest revision. The current version is always published at this page.
