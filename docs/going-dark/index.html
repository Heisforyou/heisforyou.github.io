What's Inside

Built for the games you have not watched yet

Recording it for later, stuck at work, or waiting until the house is quiet.

Hide as many games as you want

Tap the shield on any game and it is redacted: no score, no clock, no status, no play-by-play. Hide one, or hide every game you are saving for later.

The rest stays live

Every game you have not hidden keeps updating beside the ones you hid.

Six leagues

NFL, college football, MLB, NBA, college basketball, and NHL. College games can be filtered by conference.

Spoiler shield

While any game is hidden, screenshots and the app-switcher preview are blocked, so a glance at recent apps cannot leak a score.

Reveal on your terms

When you have watched a hidden game, tap it to bring that result back.

Stays on your device

Hidden games and display preferences are stored on the phone. No account, no ads, and no tracking.
